What is Orakzai Bond (OKBOND)?
Orakzai Bond (OKBOND) is a high-utility decentralized financial instrument engineered natively on the Polygon Layer-2 blockchain architecture. It represents an institutional-grade, treasury-backed decentralized bond designed to integrate asset protection with sovereign decentralized ledger technologies.
