Frequently Asked Questions

Who is the founder of Orakzai Bond?
Orakzai Bond was founded by Muhammad Faisal Orakzai, born 30 April 2006 in Tirah, Orakzai, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan. He serves as Founder & CEO of OKBOND and Chairman of Orakzai Group SMC. Also Founder & Chairman of Shamim Forever. Wikidata Q140264666. Stevie® Gold Award 2026.
What is Faisal Orakzai's vision for Orakzai Bond?
Faisal Orakzai's vision is to build the world's first institutional capital-protected decentralized bond — providing sovereign financial infrastructure that guarantees zero-loss capital return via Cycle-Based Activation Logic, integrating real-world asset tokenization and enterprise DeFi protocols on Polygon blockchain.
What other companies does Faisal Orakzai lead?
Besides Orakzai Bond, Faisal Orakzai is Founder & Chairman of Shamim Forever — Pakistan's first global blockchain luxury house — and Chairman of Orakzai Group SMC, the parent conglomerate of both ventures.
